# TicketPack privacy policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

TicketPack is maintained in the atomiteam/share-with-ai repository. Contact and privacy questions: https://github.com/atomiteam/share-with-ai/issues. Do not post confidential ticket data in public issues.

## Data accessed and purpose

When you click TicketPack, it reads the selected ticket page and may make read-only API requests to that ticket system using your existing browser session. It processes the ticket title, description, fields, comments, author information, attachment metadata and attachment files to create the ZIP you requested. Ticket fields and files can contain personal or confidential information. Page capture can include surrounding visible text; review exported content before sharing.

## Local processing and storage

TicketPack processes ticket content in browser memory and saves the requested ZIP through Chrome's download system. It does not operate a backend that receives your tickets. It does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or developer telemetry. It does not extract, store or send passwords or API tokens to its developer. Your existing session cookies may be sent by the browser to the ticket or attachment host to authorize read-only downloads.

Ticket data in a review tab remains until the tab closes. ZIP files remain on your computer until you delete them. Optional website permissions granted through Chrome remain until you revoke them in the extension settings. TicketPack does not store ticket content in Chrome storage or synchronize it between devices.

## Network requests and third parties

TicketPack contacts your selected ticket system and selected attachment hosts to retrieve content. These services receive normal network request information such as IP address and applicable session cookies. Attachment downloads can follow provider-hosted redirects. Optional host permission may be required for another file host.

If you choose the AI handoff, TicketPack copies a prompt to your clipboard and opens the selected AI app. It does not automatically upload files, paste ticket content into the AI app or send messages. If you manually attach a ZIP or other file to an AI service, that service receives the data under its own privacy policy and your organization's agreements. Clipboard contents remain until overwritten by you or another application.

## Data use commitments

The developer does not sell ticket data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to data brokers. TicketPack accesses and processes data only to export the requested ticket and support the user-initiated handoff described above. It does not use the data for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

## Controls

The action page provides Download and Share with AI. It does not display the captured ticket content. Open an individual ticket and inspect the downloaded ZIP before sharing. You can cancel a download through Chrome. AI file sharing is always manual. Revoke website permissions or uninstall TicketPack in Chrome's extension settings. Delete downloaded ZIP files separately. No account with the extension developer is required.

## Changes

Updates to this policy will be committed to the repository with an updated effective date when practices change.
